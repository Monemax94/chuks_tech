Frequently Asked Questions

How our process works and what to expect when you ask for a quote.

Services & scope

We provide network infrastructure installation and deployment, structured cabling and network connectivity, network integration and configuration, server installation and infrastructure support, hardware, software and firmware upgrades, and network operating system installation and reimaging. Link troubleshooting is available as a supporting capability.

Link troubleshooting is one of our capabilities. Describe what you are seeing through the quote form or by phone and we will discuss the next steps. Please contact us to discuss your requirements.

Our services cover new installations, upgrades, and repair or restoration work. The quote form lets you choose the project type that fits your situation.

Process

Projects follow five steps: understand requirements, plan and design, install and configure, test and verify, and handover and support.

Testing and verification is a step in our process, carried out before the completed work is handed over to you.

Equipment

We work with Cisco routers and switches, and with HP and Dell servers. Cisco, HP and Dell are trademarks of their respective owners.

We carry out hardware, software and firmware upgrades. Tell us what equipment you have and what you want to achieve, and we will discuss what is involved.

Getting a quote

Use the Request a Quote page to tell us what you need in three short steps. You can then send the summary to us by email or WhatsApp, or you can call us.

It helps to know the site or location, the approximate number of network points or devices, any equipment brands involved, your preferred timeline and a short description of the work. If you are unsure of any of this, tell us what you know.

Cost and timing depend on the scope of the work. Please contact us to discuss your requirements.
